SECURITY WITHIN TRUSTWORTHY AI

Robustness and security are part of the learning system.

Cybersecurity is one of the foundations of my trustworthy-AI work: understanding malicious, corrupted, unreliable, or privacy-sensitive participants and designing learning systems that remain useful under those conditions.

FedASL reduces the influence of unfavorable client updates under data corruption, while FedSRC moves quality-aware decisions to the client.
